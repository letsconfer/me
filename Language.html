French Resources (TCF-Specific)

1. Official Simulation Platforms (Must Use)

  • TV5MONDE: TCF Simulator - https://apprendre.tv5monde.com/en/tcf: Official broadcast partner providing hundreds of free practice questions, full-length timed simulations for Listening, Reading, and Language Structures.
  • France Éducation International (FEI) Official Resources - https://www.france-education-international.fr/en/test/tcf-tout-public: Official governing body providing downloadable candidate handbooks, sample audio tracks, and exact speaking/writing prompts.
  • RFI Savoirs: TCF Preparation - https://savoirs.rfi.fr/fr: Dedicated listening practice modules using real news audio structured like the TCF listening section.

2. Best YouTube Channels for TCF Strategy & Checklists

  • PrepMyFuture (YouTube Channel) - https://www.youtube.com/@PrepMyFutureParis: Masterclasses in TCF strategy, scoring algorithm breakdown, and time management.
  • French With Elisa / TCF Canada Strategy Videos - Memorizable templates for TCF Writing Task 1, 2, and 3.
  • En Route pour le Canada: YouTube channel dedicated to immigrants preparing for TCF Canada featuring mock speaking exams.

3. Free Study Packs & Community-Driven Question Banks

  • Reddit's TCF Shared Resource Folder - Accessible via r/learnfrench and r/ImmigrationCanada: Community-maintained resources, past TCF writing prompts, sample essays, and grammar review sheets.
  • Telegram TCF Canada Groups: Active communities (search "TCF Canada Target CLB 7" on Telegram) sharing real current exam prompts daily.
💡 How to Use These for "Exam French":

Don't waste time trying to build a massive vocabulary. Instead, use TV5MONDE daily to train your ears specifically for the TCF listening format, and grab the writing templates to write one essay a day using those exact frameworks.
